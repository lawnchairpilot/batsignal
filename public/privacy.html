Bool Signal

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

Bool Signal lets you tell your friends what you're doing right now. This page explains exactly what the app collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work, and nothing for advertising.
  • We do not sell your data, and there are no ad or analytics trackers in the app.
  • Your signals are visible only to the friends you send them to.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.

How contact matching works

When you open the Contacts tab, the app reads the phone numbers in your address book and sends them to our server to check which ones have accounts. We use those numbers to answer that one question and we do not store them. We never upload your contacts' names — those stay on your phone.

If you'd rather not do this, decline the Contacts permission. Everything else in the app keeps working, and you can still add friends by typing a phone number.

How location works

There are two separate things going on, and you control both:

  • While using the app — your location centres the map and filters signals to the ones near you. This never leaves your device unless you attach a location to a signal you create.
  • In the background — only if you grant "Always" permission and you have marked a signal as live. Your position updates for the friends on that signal so they can find you. It stops when the signal ends.

You can decline location entirely, or downgrade it in iOS Settings at any time. Signals with a typed-in place instead of a pin work without any location access.

Who else sees it

Your signals, name, and photo are visible to the friends you send them to. Beyond that, we share data with two service providers and nobody else:

  • Google Firebase hosts the app's sign-in, database, file storage, and server code. Your data lives in Google's infrastructure. See Firebase's privacy documentation.
  • Apple delivers push notifications through the Apple Push Notification service.

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose data if we are legally required to, or where we believe it's necessary to prevent harm.

How long we keep it

Your account data stays until you delete it. Individual signals stop being visible to friends once they end, but the record remains until the account is deleted or the signal is removed.

Reports are kept after the reported account is gone, since a record of enforcement is the only way to act on repeat behaviour.

Deleting your account

Open Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, your signals and their photos, your groups, your friend requests, your place in other people's friend lists, and your sign-in credentials. It cannot be undone, and it does not require emailing us.

Children

Bool Signal is not for anyone under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app, and delete everything as described above. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights over your data — contact us and we'll help.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is restricted by server-side rules so that, for example, your profile is readable only by you and your friends. No system is perfect, but we don't collect what we don't need, which is the most reliable protection available.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and notify you in the app.
