Reporting content or behaviour

Every signal, comment, and person in the app can be reported from inside the app — tap the ⋯ menu on a signal, or press and hold a person or comment, then choose Report. Reported content is hidden from you straight away and we review it within 24 hours.

You can also block someone, which ends the friendship and stops all contact between you. Undo it under Profile → Settings → Blocked.

If you can't reach the in-app tools, email us at the address above with what you saw and where.

Common questions

Why do I need to give a phone number?

It's how you sign in — there are no passwords — and it's how friends find you. It's verified by SMS code.

Do I have to share my contacts?

No. It only exists to show which of your contacts already use the app. Decline it and everything else works; you can still add friends by typing a phone number. We never upload your contacts' names, and we don't store their numbers.

Do I have to share my location?

No. Signals can use a typed-in place instead of a map pin. Background location is only ever used while you have a signal marked live, so friends can find you, and it stops when the signal ends.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → Delete Account. It removes your profile, your signals and their photos, your groups, and your sign-in credentials. It's immediate and permanent, and you don't need to email anyone.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are enabled for Bool Signal in iOS Settings. If they are and it's still quiet, email us — include your phone number so we can look at the account.
